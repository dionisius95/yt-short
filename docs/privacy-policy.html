Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026 • Contact: admin@dionesiamusik.biz.id

1. Overview

Shorts Editor ("the Application") is a local desktop software suite designed to assist content creators in processing, reframing, and publishing video content directly to user-authorized platforms including YouTube. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy.

2. Google & YouTube API Services

Shorts Editor integrates with YouTube API Services to allow users to authenticate and upload videos directly to their YouTube channels. The Application requests only the minimal scopes required to perform these functions:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
Used strictly to upload user-rendered video clips and Shorts directly to the user's YouTube channel upon the user's explicit command.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
Used to retrieve channel profile information (such as channel title and avatar) to display and confirm the active target account in the user interface.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email
Used to display the connected account email within the Application settings.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local-First Architecture: All OAuth tokens, API keys, local credentials, video files, subtitles, and configuration metadata are stored exclusively on your local device.
  • No Central Server Tracking: We do not operate external intermediate servers to collect, intercept, or log your Google user data or credentials. API transactions occur directly between your device and Google/YouTube API endpoints.
  • No Data Sharing or Sale: We do not sell, rent, or distribute any user data or Google account information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Editor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Third-Party Links & Terms

By connecting and using YouTube integration features within Shorts Editor, you acknowledge and agree that your usage is also subject to:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Revocation & Data Deletion

You have full control over your credentials and authorizations:

  • In-App Disconnect: You can disconnect your YouTube account anytime via Settings → Disconnect, which immediately deletes stored OAuth tokens from your local database.
  • Google Security Settings: You can revoke the Application's access at any time directly from Google Account Permissions.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: admin@dionesiamusik.biz.id
